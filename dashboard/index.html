About dallas-intel

Daily property-intelligence pipeline for Dallas County, Texas. Scrapes the County Clerk's Official Public Records, foreclosure notices, and DCAD parcel data; enriches and scores records to surface motivated-seller signals.

Source code, architecture rationale, and full decision ledger live in the project repo. The authoritative design document is docs/ARCHITECTURE.md.

Operates on public records only. All scoring is derivative analysis applied to records that are public under Texas Government Code Ch. 552 (Public Information Act).
